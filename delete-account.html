Request account deletion

You can also request account and data deletion directly inside the Belive / Unilive app:
Settings > Account > Delete Account

If you have uninstalled the app, use this form to request deletion of your account and all associated data.

By submitting, you confirm that you want to permanently delete your account, including your profile, wallet balance, posts, messages, live history, and all associated personal data. We process deletion requests within 30 days.
